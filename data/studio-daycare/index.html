    How the score is built

    Each daycare gets up to 100. The sliders are the points available for each question. A perfect fit on that question takes all of those points; a poor fit takes few. The default puts English first, then whether a 15-month-old belongs there, whether Vantaa will actually offer the place, whether the fee stays near the municipal cap, and how long the trip is.
