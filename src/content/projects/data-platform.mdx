## Overview

A production analytics platform that ingests millions of product events per day, transforms
them with **dbt**, and exposes trustworthy metrics through self-serve dashboards — with
freshness monitoring baked in.

## The problem

Stakeholders didn't trust the numbers: pipelines failed silently, metrics drifted between
teams, and "is this data fresh?" had no clear answer.

## What I built

- A streaming **ingest** layer (Kafka) feeding the warehouse in near real time.
- A modeled **semantic layer** in dbt with tests and documented metrics.
- **Freshness SLAs** with alerting when a model goes stale.
- Self-serve dashboards so teams answer their own questions.

## Architecture

Sources flow through ingest into the warehouse, where dbt builds staging, fact, and mart
models that power the dashboards.

## Impact

- **Under 5 min** end-to-end data freshness for core models.
- **100+** documented, tested metrics in the semantic layer.
- Reduced ad-hoc data requests to the team by **70%**.
