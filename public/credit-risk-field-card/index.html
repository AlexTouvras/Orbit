Where judgement stays

  1. A paying account is not low risk. The installment can arrive while the risk since origination is already up.
  2. A score is not the decision. Policy, the cutoff, and the person who may override are.
  3. An extra amount needs an exit. If the stage, the parameters, and that owner cannot be named, the number does not get booked.
