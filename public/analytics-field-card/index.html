Real-world example

Credit risk analytics

Which customer segments are driving the increase in expected credit losses?

Illustrative scenario, not a claim about observed results.

  1. 01 Question

    Which customer segments are driving the increase in expected credit losses?

  2. 02 Grain

    Define the reporting unit, reporting date, portfolio scope and treatment of duplicates.

  3. 03 Truth

    Apply approved PD, LGD and EAD definitions, validate the calculations and reconcile totals.

  4. 04 Decision

    Expose the validated segment-level drivers in a scorecard that supports investigation and action.

When each trial is a slow PD or ECL backtest, that search lives on the Bayesian optimisation card. Grid the cheap notebook. Model the expensive pack.

Tools and technologies

The right tools for the job

  • Data platform. Databricks, DuckDB, MotherDuck. Snowflake sits in the same lane when the warehouse is the constraint.
  • Transformation. dbt, Python, Jupyter. Spark when the volume requires it.
  • Semantic and BI. Semantic layer, Power BI, Looker. Tableau when that is the room’s consume tool.
  • Orchestration. Airflow or Fabric schedules the path. It does not define the number.
  • Monitoring. Great Expectations for tests. OpenLineage for where the number came from.

Common anti-patterns

Avoid these traps

  • ×Single source of truth with no reconciliation.
  • ×Hidden definitions that mean different things.
  • ×Unvalidated data and no quality checks.
  • ×Dashboard-only thinking with no decision.
  • ×No lineage and no way to trace the number.

Maturity ladder

Build over time

  1. 1Ad hocManual, inconsistent, low trust.
  2. 2DefinedStandards and basic governance.
  3. 3ManagedAutomated, monitored, reconciled.
  4. 4OptimizedPredictive, real-time, self-service.

If you can’t name the grain, the owner, and how to stop a bad publish, you don’t ship the number.
