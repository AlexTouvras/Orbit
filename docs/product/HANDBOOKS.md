## Editorial contract

Every page:

1. Starts from the decision or failure the reader has, then the subject. The opening of Data that drives decisions starts from the capability instead: the path a number walks when a person can act. A failure is not the first thing on that page.
2. Says why the practice works, what it assumes, and when another approach fits.
3. Gives a procedure, a decision, a check, or another artifact the reader can use.
4. Separates established guidance, the author's rule, and a hypothesis. A synthetic scene says so in its first line.
5. Adds something the other pages do not already say.

A written chapter ends once. The last section is the author's check against a cheap draft: why this chapter's practice applies now, and why a faster draft does not retire it. The reason belongs to that chapter. A claim that fails the check is rewritten where it lives. The check is not a survey of tools.

One thesis per page. Headings follow the subject. They are not a fixed list of eight. A page is as long as the argument: a principle can be short, a method needs the day and the limit, a deep dive needs the mechanism. Do not add length to look finished. On screen that argument scrolls.

Voice: direct, operational, first person for the author's judgment. Define a term a stranger may not know. No corporate filler. A memorable sentence is rare, and it has to clarify the rule.

Evidence: cite the primary source, with its date or version, and only for a claim that source makes. A small number sits at the end of the section that uses the work, and opens that work in the reference list. The list opens the source. A link to another chapter stays the chapter's name. The page ends with one numbered list of the works. The number scrolls to that work. Do not invent a metric, a client, or a result. A number you did not open does not go on the page. Say how you would tell the practice is working. That is a measure to track, not a result you do not have. Put the date you last checked the claims on the page.

A figure is allowed when it carries the procedure: a loop, a decision, a shape. A photograph is allowed when it draws that section and states no amount the prose does not. It belongs to that book. The words still work if the figure or the photograph does not. No motion required to understand the page. The standard is the contract above. The diagrams, the section count, and the length change with the subject.

A picture does not sit behind the words. A narrative picture shows what that section says, in words a reader can use. Those are the pictures that matter. What good looks like is the test. The figure is the operating model: Decision, Definition, Technology, Use, and Review. Ask, Grain, Truth, and Use sit inside the definition. The review is separate. Technology supports the practice. Later pages of this book still use their current pictures until they are revised the same way.
