# Product North Star

Orbit (alextouvras.com) is a professional operating system, not a conventional portfolio.

## Four questions

| Layer | Route | Answers |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **HUd** | `/card` | **WHO IS ALEX?** |
| **Hub** | `/` (+ Portfolio, Writes, desks) | **WHAT DOES ALEX BUILD AND HOW DOES HE THINK?** |
| **Studio** | `/studio` | **WHERE IS ALEX GOING?** |
| **Career timeline** | About / future — not a new Foundation page | **HOW DID ALEX GET HERE?** |

## Journey

**Card → curiosity → Hub → evidence.**

The card is a 15–30 second identity scan. It must not carry the whole professional story. Hub and deeper surfaces carry evidence.

## Locked identity (owner-owned)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **ARCHETYPE** | AI & Data Systems Lead |
| **STATEMENT** | Building and leading data & AI systems that solve complex business problems. |
| **CHAIN** | Business problems → data → intelligent systems → delivery → measurable outcomes |
| **Domain** | Financial services = initial advantage, not permanent identity |

Agents may suggest layout and implementation. They do not invent identity language. Propagate only after owner Gate 0 approval.

## Decision principle

**Capability → Evidence → Public Proof → Opportunity**

## Product vs implementation

Docs decide product. Agents pick implementation only inside that box.

## Foundation vs later

**Orbit 2.0 Foundation** = infrastructure (product truth, positioning, Studio tracker, HUD NOW). It does **not** create new career achievements or labs.
